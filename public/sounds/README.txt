Sound-board sounds: drop .mp3 files here.
The file name becomes the label: drum-roll.mp3 shows as "Drum Roll".
Use letters, numbers, - and _ in names. Keep them short (a few seconds) and small.

victory.mp3 and defeat.mp3 are special: the game plays them on the results screen
(victory for everyone who emptied their hand, defeat for whoever was left holding cards).
They never appear on the sound board.
